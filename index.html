LET'S KEEP IT SIMPLE

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

The things farmers ask us most.

Is Harvest Booster the same as Liquid Compost?
Yes. Harvest Booster is the new name and packaging for OrganicPlus Liquid Compost. It is the same OrganicPlus product. Existing farmer photos and testimonials may still show or mention Liquid Compost.
Do I still use my usual fertiliser or manure?
Yes. Use Harvest Booster alongside your usual fertiliser or manure programme. It gives your soil and crops an extra boost; it does not replace the full nutrition your crop needs.
Which crops can I use it on?
Farmers use OrganicPlus Liquid Compost, now Harvest Booster, on fruiting crops such as watermelon, chillies and eggplant, as well as vegetables, dalo, cassava, ginger, yaqona and fruit trees. Follow the label and the guidance for your crop, especially for seedlings and young plants.
How much do I mix, and how often do I apply?
Check the directions on your pack and follow the programme recommended for your crop. The strength and timing depend on the crop, its growth stage and whether you are applying to soil or leaves. Use the correct measure each time.
Should I apply it to the soil or the leaves?
Follow the label for your application method. For soil application, apply around the plants' root zone. Leaf sprays may need a different dilution. Avoid spraying in the hottest part of the day.
How much will I need for my farm?
This depends on the number of plants, how much mixed solution you apply to each plant and how many applications your crop needs. Have your crop, plant count and spray-tank size ready when asking OrganicPlus for a crop programme.
Where can I buy Harvest Booster?
Use the store finder above to find an OrganicPlus retailer in your area. Call the store before travelling to confirm stock and pack sizes. During the packaging change, some stores may carry the previous Liquid Compost pack.
