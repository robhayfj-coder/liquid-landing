LET’S KEEP IT SIMPLE

Good questions.

Is this the same as Liquid Compost?
Yes. Harvest Booster is the new name and packaging for OrganicPlus Liquid Compost. Our farmer photos and testimonials may still show or mention the previous name.
When should I start, and how often?
For flowering and fruiting crops, start when the first flowers appear. Apply every 7 days until harvesting finishes. Follow the mixing and application directions on your pack.
Do I keep using my fertiliser or manure?
Yes. Use Harvest Booster alongside your usual fertiliser or manure programme. It supports your crop and soil, while your normal programme supplies the nutrition your crop needs.
Which crops can I use it on?
Harvest Booster supports flowering and fruiting crops, including watermelon, chillies, eggplant and fruit trees. For root crops, ginger, yaqona and other crop programmes, ask OrganicPlus for guidance on timing and application.
How much do I mix with water?
Use the dilution and application directions on your pack. Rates depend on your application method and crop. Have your crop, plant count and tank size ready when asking for advice.
Where can I buy it, and what does it cost?
Use our store finder to find a stockist in your area. Call to check today’s price, available pack sizes and stock before travelling.
