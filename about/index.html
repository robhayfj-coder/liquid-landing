GET TO KNOW ORGANICPLUS

Better farming.
Made simple.

Our journey began in Fiji in 2020. Today, we bring agricultural science and practical farming together through biological crop solutions.

Local resources.
A useful next life.

OrganicPlus began with a practical idea: food-production by-products could become useful inputs for farming. Through a controlled biological production process, we turn these resources into crop solutions designed to work alongside normal fertiliser or manure.

Our approach combines product development with farmer feedback, clear instructions and local guidance. Farmers should understand what a product does, know when to use it and feel confident making it part of their routine.

Harvest Booster supports flowering and fruiting, starting at first flowers. Growth Booster is our upcoming addition for early crop development and green, leafy growth.

Our positioning is expressed in a simple promise: Better farming made simple. It brings together agricultural science, practical use, real farmer experiences and access through stockists across Fiji.
